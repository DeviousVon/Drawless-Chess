# Privacy Policy for Drawless Chess

**Effective date:** July 11, 2026

**Last updated:** August 14, 2026

BB_Games ("we," "us," or "our") publishes Drawless Chess for iPhone and iPad
under Apple bundle identifier `com.drawlesschess` and for Android under package
name `com.drawlesschess`. This policy explains how the mobile apps handle
information.

## Privacy at a glance

Drawless Chess is an offline, single-player chess game. The mobile apps:

- do not require an account or sign-in;
- do not collect or transmit personal information to BB_Games;
- do not contain advertising, analytics, tracking, developer-operated
  crash-reporting, or in-app purchase SDKs; and
- do not use developer-operated network services or request location, camera,
  microphone, contacts, or shared-storage access. The Android app also declares
  no internet permission.

## Information kept on your device

To provide gameplay and Resume, Drawless Chess stores app data in the private
container assigned to it by iOS, iPadOS, or Android. This can include:

- the current or most recently saved game, board position, moves, result, clock
  state, chosen rules, player side, opponent level, and counts of gameplay
  actions such as hints, undos, and pauses;
- a random device-local player identifier and completed-game history used to
  calculate wins, losses, streaks, unassisted wins, and average game score; and
- app preferences such as the visual theme, coordinates, sound and haptic
  settings, presentation choices, and whether the introductory rules guide has
  been dismissed.

This information is used only to run the game, restore a saved game, show local
player statistics, and remember preferences. The local player identifier is not
an account, is not linked to an online identity, and is not sent to BB_Games or
to an in-app third-party service.

## Device and cloud backup

Depending on your device and backup settings, Apple device or iCloud Backup, or
Android's system backup, may include some locally stored Drawless Chess data.
Those backups are performed and controlled by Apple, Google, your device
manufacturer, or another backup provider—not by Drawless Chess. BB_Games does
not receive, access, or control the backed-up data.

You can manage or delete backups through your Apple, Google, device, or backup-
provider settings. The provider determines its exact controls and retention
period.

## Network access and external services

Drawless Chess does not send gameplay, preferences, identifiers, or statistics
to BB_Games. Its Open source and Privacy screens contain fixed links. If you
choose one, the operating system opens an external browser or another app you
select. That external app and the destination website process the visit under
their own privacy policies; Drawless Chess does not receive information about
it.

If you install or purchase Drawless Chess through Apple's App Store or Google
Play, the store provider processes download, transaction, device, and related
platform information under its own terms. BB_Games does not receive your
payment-card or bank details. Apple or Google may provide developers with store
reports such as aggregate installation statistics and platform-generated
performance or crash diagnostics. Those store services are not data transmitted
by Drawless Chess itself.

## Sharing and sale of information

Drawless Chess does not transmit personal information or app activity to
BB_Games, so we do not sell it or share it with third parties. The apps have no
advertising network, user profiling, cross-app tracking, or developer-operated
server.

## Retention and deletion

An in-progress saved game remains on the device until it is completed,
forfeited, or the app's data is removed. Completed-game history, local
statistics, and preferences remain until the app's data is removed.

- **iPhone or iPad:** delete Drawless Chess and its data using **Delete App**
  from the Home Screen or from **Settings > General > iPhone Storage / iPad
  Storage > Drawless Chess**. Apple's **Offload App** option retains documents
  and data and therefore is not the same as deleting the app.
- **Android:** use **Settings > Apps > Drawless Chess > Storage > Clear
  storage/data**, or uninstall Drawless Chess. Exact labels vary by device.

Deleting installed app data may not immediately remove a separate device or
cloud backup. Manage those copies through your backup-provider settings.
Because Drawless Chess has no user accounts or developer-operated data store,
BB_Games has no server-side account or gameplay record to locate or delete.

## Children

Drawless Chess is not specifically directed to children under 13 and does not
knowingly collect personal information from children or adults. A parent or
guardian with a privacy question can contact us at the address below.

## Security

Local data is protected by the app sandbox and security settings of your Apple
or Android device. No storage method can be guaranteed completely secure, so
keep your device, operating system, and screen lock up to date.

## Changes to this policy

If Drawless Chess changes how it handles information, we will update this
policy and its "Last updated" date before or when the relevant app change is
released. Material changes will also be disclosed through the app or store
listing when appropriate.
