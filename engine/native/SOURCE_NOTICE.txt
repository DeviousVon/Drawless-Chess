The exact Drawless modifications are recorded by the ordered patch series under
engine/patches/. Reproduction metadata is under engine/native/. Android builds
use android/engine/ and the JNI bridge; iOS builds use ios-engine/,
multiplatform/, iosApp/, and scripts/build-ios-engine.sh with the static Apple
bridge. The Android AAR packages upstream Copying.txt, AUTHORS, this notice, the
source locks, and the Drawless patch series. The iOS application packages this
notice and the project, Apache, and third-party license resources.

The WebAssembly proof of concept is a different artifact and is not the native
source pin. Its independent package identity is recorded in
engine/native/wasm-poc.properties.
